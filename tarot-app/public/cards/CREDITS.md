# Card art credits

Pamela Colman Smith, illustrations for the Rider-Waite tarot, original 1909 "Roses & Lilies" printing, scanned by Saskia Jansen. From Wikimedia Commons, category "Rider-Waite tarot deck (Roses & Lilies)". Saved as WebP for the app at about 800 to 825 px wide (some came from Commons' 800 px thumbnails, the rest from the full scans); no other changes.
